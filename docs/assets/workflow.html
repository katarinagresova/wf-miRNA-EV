5

Read classification

one class for each read, for each miRNA database
  1. 1Best hit. The hit with the fewest mismatches over all small RNA references gives the class. On a tie, a fixed order decides (miRNA, rRNA, tRNA, …).
  2. 2miRNA. 16–28 nt, the read ends near the ends of the mature miRNA, 0 mismatches for 18 nt or shorter. 1–3 non-templated 3′ nt are allowed.
  3. 3Genome. A read without a small RNA hit gets the class of the genome annotation on the same strand.
  4. 4Contaminant. 18 nt or longer, and fewer mismatches than the best human hit. Looser rules for reads with weak human evidence.
  5. 5No full-length hit. The 25-nt ends (up to 1 mismatch), else the 18-nt ends (exact), give the class: rRNA, tRNA, ncRNA or contaminant.
